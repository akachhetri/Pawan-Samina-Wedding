Traditional Attire & Sacred Customs

Traditional Dress Code

Daura Suruwal with Dhaka Topi, Red/Crimson & Gold Sarees, Lehenga, Kurta, or formal evening attire in vibrant auspicious tones.

Sacred Mandap Etiquette

Please remove footwear before stepping onto the sacred Vivaha Mandap area, and feel free to shower flower petals (Akshata & Ful) during Saptapadi.
